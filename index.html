About

What I do

I'm a Data Scientist specializing in NLP and applied AI at production scale. At Movistar (Telefónica Hispam), I design and operate AI systems that handle over +300,000 customer messages per week, combining rule-based classification (Regex/JSON) with LLMs to automate intent detection in natural language.

My focus is on building systems that actually run in production — not just notebooks. I work across the full pipeline: data ingestion with PySpark on Databricks, model design, prompt engineering, and business dashboards in Power BI.

Currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Data Science at Universidad Siglo 21 and open to NLP Engineering, AI/ML Engineering, and Data Science roles globally.
